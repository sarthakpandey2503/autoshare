Common questions

Who can use AutoShare? ▾

Only IIIT Kottayam students with a valid @iiitkottayam.ac.in email. Sign up once with your username, gender, and phone — then log in anytime with a one-time code.

How does fare splitting work? ▾

The host enters the total auto fare. Per-head cost is calculated automatically as people join — up to 4 riders per pool.

What does the host see when someone requests to join? ▾

Your username, gender, and phone number — so they can call and confirm before approving. Your details aren't shown on the public board.

Can I be in more than one ride at a time? ▾

You can relate to up to 2 rides (offered, joined, or pending request), but departure times must be at least 30 minutes apart.
